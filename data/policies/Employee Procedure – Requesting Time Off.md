# Employee Procedure – Requesting Time Off

## Purpose
This procedure outlines the steps employees must follow when requesting Paid Time Off (PTO), sick leave, or other approved absences.

---

# 1. Review PTO Balance

Before submitting a request:

1. Log into the TimeOff system.
2. Review available PTO balance.
3. Confirm accrual availability for the requested dates.

Employees should ensure sufficient accrued PTO is available before submitting vacation requests.

---

# 2. Determine Type of Leave

Employees should identify the type of leave being requested:

| Leave Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Vacation PTO | Vacations, personal days |
| Sick Leave | Illness, appointments, mental health |
| Protected Leave | CFRA, FMLA, pregnancy leave |
| Jury Duty | Court-mandated service |
| Bereavement | Death of family member |

If uncertain, contact Human Resources.

---

# 3. Discuss Plans with Manager

Employees should notify their manager as early as possible regarding planned absences.

Recommended notice:
- 2 weeks minimum for vacations over 3 days
- 30 days for extended leave whenever possible
- Same-day notice for illness/emergency

---

# 4. Submit PTO Request

Employees must submit requests through the TimeOff system.

Required information:
- Requested dates
- Total hours requested
- Type of leave
- Coverage plan if applicable

---

# 5. Manager Review Process

Managers will review requests based on:
- Staffing needs
- Business priorities
- Team coverage
- Existing approved absences

Managers may:
- Approve
- Deny
- Request modified dates

---

# 6. Employee Notification

Employees will receive notification through the TimeOff once approved or denied.

Employees should not finalize travel arrangements until PTO approval is received.

---

# 7. Extended Leave Requests

Employees requesting protected or extended leave should:
1. Contact HR directly
2. Complete required leave documentation
3. Provide supporting documentation if required

HR will provide additional instructions.

---

# 8. Sick Leave Procedures

If absent due to illness:
1. Notify manager as soon as possible
2. Update manager daily if absence extends beyond one day
3. Provide documentation if required under policy or law

---

# 9. PTO During Hybrid or Remote Work

Employees working hybrid or remote schedules must still submit PTO requests for:
- Full-day absences
- Partial-day absences
- Medical appointments during work hours

---

# 10. Questions

Questions regarding leave eligibility or PTO balances should be directed to Human Resources.
