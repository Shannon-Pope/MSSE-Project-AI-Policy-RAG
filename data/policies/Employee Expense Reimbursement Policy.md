# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy – California Employees

## Purpose
This policy outlines reimbursement procedures for approved business expenses incurred by employees while conducting company business.

The company complies with California Labor Code Section 2802 regarding reimbursement of necessary business expenses.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all California employees.

The company operates primarily on-site in San Diego, California, with approved hybrid work arrangements available.

---

## General Guidelines
All business expenses must be:
- Necessary for business purposes
- Reasonable and cost-conscious
- Properly documented
- Submitted within required timelines

---

## Reimbursable Expenses

### Business Travel
Approved travel-related expenses include:
- Airfare
- Hotel accommodations
- Ground transportation
- Parking and tolls
- Mileage reimbursement
- Meals

### Hybrid & Remote Work
Employees approved for hybrid work may be reimbursed for necessary business expenses, including:
- Approved office equipment
- Required software
- Business-related internet or communication expenses when applicable under California law

---

## Meal Allowance
Employees traveling for business are eligible for reimbursement up to:

- $125 per person, per day

This amount includes:
- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner
- Reasonable tips

Alcohol is not reimbursable unless approved for a client or company event.

---

## Hotel Allowance
Hotel reimbursement is limited to:

- $200 per night before taxes and fees

Exceptions require prior management approval.

Employees are expected to select safe, reasonably priced accommodations.

---

## Airfare
Employees should book:
- Economy class airfare
- Lowest practical logical fare

Upgrades require executive approval.

---

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Examples include:
- Personal entertainment
- Traffic violations
- Personal travel expenses
- Unapproved upgrades
- Expenses without receipts

---

## Expense Submission
Employees must submit:
- Itemized receipts
- Business justification
- Travel dates
- Attendee names if applicable

Expenses should be submitted within 30 days.

---

## Approval Process
Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving expenses for policy compliance.

Finance will process approved reimbursements according to payroll schedules.

---

## Fraudulent Claims
Intentional misuse or falsification of expenses may result in disciplinary action up to termination.

---

## Questions
Questions regarding reimbursements should be directed to Finance or Human Resources.
