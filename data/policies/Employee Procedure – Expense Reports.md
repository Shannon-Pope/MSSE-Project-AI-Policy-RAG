# Employee Procedure – Creating & Submitting Expense Reports

## Purpose
This procedure explains how employees should create, document, and submit expense reimbursement requests.

---

# 1. Confirm Expense Eligibility

Before submitting an expense:

1. Verify the expense complies with company policy.
2. Confirm the expense was necessary for business purposes.
3. Ensure required approvals were obtained.

Examples of reimbursable expenses:
- Approved travel
- Client meetings
- Business meals
- Parking/tolls
- Approved office supplies

---

# 2. Collect Required Documentation

Employees must retain:
- Itemized receipts
- Hotel folios
- Flight confirmations
- Mileage records
- Business purpose notes

Credit card statements alone are not sufficient.

---

# 3. Log Into Expense Management System

Employees should access the ExpenseIt expense management system.

---

# 4. Create a New Expense Report

Select:
- “Create New Report”
- Appropriate reporting period
- Business purpose category

Examples:
- Client Meeting
- Sales Travel
- Recruiting
- Training & Development

---

# 5. Add Expense Entries

For each expense, employees must enter:

| Required Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Date | 05/10/2026 |
| Vendor | Marriott |
| Expense Type | Hotel |
| Amount | $189.00 |
| Business Purpose | Customer meeting in Los Angeles |

---

# 6. Upload Receipts

Attach itemized receipts for all required expenses.

Receipt requirements:
- Clear and readable
- Show vendor name
- Show transaction amount
- Show transaction date

---

# 7. Verify Policy Limits

Employees must ensure:
- Daily meal allowance does not exceed $125/day
- Hotel rate does not exceed $200/night unless approved
- Airfare complies with economy class requirements

---

# 8. Submit Report

Before submitting:
1. Review all entries
2. Verify receipts are attached
3. Confirm accounting categories are correct

Select “Submit.”

---

# 9. Manager Approval Process

Managers review reports for:
- Policy compliance
- Business justification
- Receipt completeness
- Budget considerations

Managers may:
- Approve
- Reject
- Return for corrections

---

# 10. Finance Processing

Once approved:
1. Finance reviews the report
2. Reimbursement is processed through payroll or ACH payment

Processing timelines may vary.

---

# 11. Common Submission Errors

Avoid:
- Missing receipts
- Duplicate expenses
- Personal expenses
- Incorrect categories
- Exceeding policy limits

---

# 12. Questions

Questions regarding expense reports should be directed to Finance or Human Resources.
