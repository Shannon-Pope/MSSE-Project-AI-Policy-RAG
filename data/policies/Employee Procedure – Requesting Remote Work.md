# Employee Procedure – Requesting Flexible Remote Work

## Purpose
This procedure outlines how employees may request hybrid or flexible remote work arrangements.

The company operates primarily on-site in San Diego, California. Flexible remote work arrangements may be approved based on business and operational needs.

---

# 1. Review Eligibility

Employees should first determine whether their role is appropriate for remote or hybrid work.

Factors considered include:
- Job responsibilities
- Customer interaction requirements
- Team collaboration needs
- Performance history
- Security requirements

---

# 2. Discuss Request with Manager

Employees should schedule a discussion with their manager before submitting a formal request.

Discussion topics should include:
- Requested schedule
- Business justification
- Team collaboration impact
- Communication expectations
- Productivity considerations

---

# 3. Submit Formal Request

Employees must submit a written request through the HR or manager approval process.

The request should include:

| Required Information | Example |
|---|---|
| Requested Schedule | Remote Fridays |
| Start Date | June 1, 2026 |
| Business Justification | Increased focus time |
| Workspace Description | Dedicated home office |

---

# 4. Workspace Requirements

Employees requesting remote work must confirm:
- Reliable internet access
- Safe work environment
- Professional meeting capability
- Ability to maintain confidentiality

---

# 5. Security Requirements

Employees must agree to:
- Use company-issued equipment
- Enable MFA
- Use VPN access if required
- Protect confidential information

---

# 6. Management Review

Managers will review:
- Business impact
- Team coverage
- Performance history
- Operational requirements

Managers may:
- Approve
- Deny
- Modify requested schedules

---

# 7. Human Resources Review

HR may review requests for:
- Policy compliance
- California employment considerations
- Payroll/tax implications
- Equipment requirements

---

# 8. Employee Notification

Employees will receive written confirmation of:
- Approved schedule
- Start date
- Conditions of approval
- Duration if temporary

---

# 9. Ongoing Expectations

Approved employees must:
- Maintain productivity
- Attend required meetings
- Be available during working hours
- Follow all company policies

Remote work approval may be modified or revoked if expectations are not met.

---

# 10. Temporary Remote Work Requests

Employees needing temporary flexibility should:
1. Notify their manager as early as possible
2. Provide expected duration
3. Ensure work responsibilities can still be met

---

# 11. Working Outside California

Employees may not work permanently outside California without prior HR approval.

---

# 12. Questions

Questions regarding hybrid or remote work arrangements should be directed to Human Resources.
