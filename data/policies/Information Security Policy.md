# Information Security Policy – California Operations

## Purpose
This policy establishes security standards for protecting company systems, confidential information, intellectual property, employee information, and customer data.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to:
- Employees
- Contractors
- Consultants
- Temporary workers
- Approved third parties

All employees are based in California and primarily work on-site in San Diego, with approved hybrid work arrangements available.

---

## Acceptable Use
Company systems and devices are intended primarily for authorized business purposes.

Employees may not:
- Access unauthorized systems
- Share confidential information improperly
- Install unauthorized software
- Circumvent security controls

---

## Password & Authentication Requirements
Employees must:
- Use strong passwords
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Never share login credentials
- Immediately report compromised accounts

Approved password managers are encouraged.

---

## Device Security
Employees must:
- Lock devices when unattended
- Keep systems updated
- Use approved antivirus and security software
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately

---

## Hybrid & Remote Work Security
Employees working remotely or in hybrid arrangements must:
- Use secure internet connections
- Avoid unsecured public Wi-Fi
- Use VPN access when required
- Protect confidential discussions and materials

---

## Data Classification

### Public
Approved for public release.

### Internal
Internal operational information.

### Confidential
Sensitive business or employee information.

### Restricted
Highly sensitive information requiring enhanced protection.

---

## Confidentiality Obligations
Employees are responsible for protecting confidential and proprietary information during and after employment.

Unauthorized disclosure may result in disciplinary action or legal consequences.

---

## Security Awareness
Employees are required to complete periodic security awareness and phishing training.

---

## Incident Reporting
Employees must immediately report:
- Data breaches
- Suspicious emails
- Unauthorized access attempts
- Lost devices
- Security incidents

Reports should be directed to the IT or Security team.

---

## Monitoring
The company reserves the right to monitor company systems and communications in accordance with California and federal law.

---

## Violations
Violations of this policy may result in:
- Loss of access privileges
- Disciplinary action
- Termination
- Legal action

---

## Questions
Questions regarding information security should be directed to the IT or Security team.
