# Employee Procedure – Creating & Submitting Travel Requests

## Purpose
This procedure outlines the process employees must follow when requesting approval for business travel.

---

# 1. Determine Business Need

Business travel should only be requested when necessary for:
- Customer meetings
- Conferences
- Training
- Recruiting
- Team collaboration
- Business operations

Employees should first evaluate whether virtual participation is possible.

---

# 2. Discuss Travel with Manager

Before booking travel:
1. Discuss purpose of trip
2. Confirm budget availability
3. Review timing and staffing impacts

Manager approval is required before reservations are made.

---

# 3. Create Travel Request

Employees must submit a formal travel request through the EmployeeTravel system.

Required information includes:

| Required Information | Example |
|---|---|
| Destination | Austin, TX |
| Travel Dates | June 10–13 |
| Business Purpose | Customer workshop |
| Estimated Cost | $1,250 |
| Attendees | Shannon Pope |

---

# 4. Estimate Travel Costs

Employees should estimate:
- Airfare
- Hotel
- Meals
- Ground transportation
- Parking/tolls

Policy limits:
- Hotel maximum: $200/night before taxes
- Meal allowance: $125/day

---

# 5. Obtain Approvals

Travel requests route through:
1. Direct manager
2. Department leadership if required
3. Finance if needed

Employees should not make reservations until approval is received.

---

# 6. Book Travel

Once approved:
- Book economy airfare. Flights over 4 hours are eligible for premium seating. Flights over 8 hours are eligible for business class seating. 
- Select reasonably priced hotels
- Use preferred vendors if applicable

Employees should prioritize cost-effective options.

---

# 7. Travel Documentation

Employees should retain:
- Flight confirmations
- Hotel receipts
- Transportation receipts
- Conference registration documentation

These documents are required for expense reimbursement.

---

# 8. Changes to Travel Plans

Employees must notify their manager if:
- Dates change
- Costs increase significantly
- Travel is canceled

Additional approval may be required.

---

# 9. International Travel

International travel may require:
- Executive approval (flights > $2,000)
- Passport validation
- Visa documentation
- Security review

Employees should coordinate with HR and leadership well in advance.

---

# 10. Travel Safety Expectations

Employees should:
- Follow local laws
- Use reputable transportation
- Exercise good judgment
- Report emergencies immediately

---

# 11. Expense Reporting After Travel

Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of trip completion.

Refer to the Expense Report Procedure document.

---

# 12. Questions

Questions regarding travel approval or booking should be directed to Finance or Human Resources.
