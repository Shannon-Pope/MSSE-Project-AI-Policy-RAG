# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy – California Employees

## Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to provide paid time off benefits that support employee wellbeing, work-life balance, and compliance with California employment laws.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees based in California.

The company operates primarily on-site in San Diego, California. Eligible employees may participate in hybrid work arrangements with manager approval.

---

## Eligibility

### Full-Time Employees
Full-time employees are eligible to accrue PTO beginning on their first day of employment.

### Part-Time Employees
Part-time employees are also eligible to accrue PTO, but at a lower accrual rate than full-time employees.

---

## PTO Accrual Rates

### Full-Time Employees
Full-time employees accrue PTO at the following rate:

- 1.54 hours per pay period
- Equivalent to approximately 20 days annually

### Part-Time Employees
Part-time employees accrue PTO proportionally based on scheduled work hours.

Accrual rates and balances will be reflected in payroll or HR systems.

---

## PTO Usage
PTO may be used for:
- Vacation
- Personal time
- Illness or medical appointments
- Mental health and wellness
- Family care responsibilities

Employees may begin using accrued PTO after accrual begins, subject to manager approval and business needs.

---

## California Sick Leave Compliance
Where required under California law, employees will receive paid sick leave benefits that meet or exceed state and local requirements.

The company complies with:
- California Healthy Workplaces, Healthy Families Act
- Applicable San Diego paid sick leave requirements

---

## Company Holidays
The company observes the following holidays:

- New Year’s Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day

Additional floating holidays may be announced annually.

---

## Requesting PTO
Employees should:
1. Submit PTO requests through the HR system.
2. Provide advance notice whenever possible:
   - 2 weeks for vacations longer than 3 business days
   - 30 days for extended leave requests
3. Coordinate coverage with their manager.

Approval is based on staffing and business needs.

---

## PTO Carryover
Unused accrued PTO may carry over in accordance with California law and company limits.

The company may establish a reasonable accrual cap consistent with California regulations.

---

## Separation of Employment
Accrued unused PTO will be paid out upon separation as required by California law.

---

## Leave of Absence
Employees may qualify for protected leave under:
- California Family Rights Act (CFRA)
- Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)
- Pregnancy Disability Leave (PDL)
- Other applicable laws

---

## Questions
Questions regarding PTO or leave rights should be directed to Human Resources.
