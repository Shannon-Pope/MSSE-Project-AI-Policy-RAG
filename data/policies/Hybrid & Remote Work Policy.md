# Hybrid & Remote Work Policy – California Employees

## Purpose
This policy establishes expectations for hybrid and remote work arrangements while supporting collaboration, flexibility, productivity, and compliance with California employment laws.

---

## Company Work Model
The company operates primarily on-site in San Diego, California.

Employees may be eligible for hybrid or flexible remote work arrangements with management approval.

Remote work arrangements are not guaranteed and may be modified based on business needs.

---

## Eligibility
Hybrid or remote work eligibility depends on:
- Job responsibilities
- Performance
- Team collaboration requirements
- Operational needs
- Security considerations

Managers and Human Resources determine eligibility.

---

## Hybrid Schedule Expectations
Approved employees may work remotely on designated days while maintaining regular in-office attendance expectations.

Employees are expected to:
- Maintain reliable communication
- Attend required meetings
- Be available during working hours
- Fulfill performance expectations

---

## Flexible Remote Work Arrangements
Flexible remote work arrangements may be approved on a temporary or ongoing basis.

Examples include:
- Temporary remote work during personal circumstances
- Alternate hybrid schedules
- Occasional work-from-home flexibility

Management approval is required.

---

## Workspace Requirements
Employees working remotely must maintain:
- A safe and professional workspace
- Reliable internet access
- Appropriate confidentiality protections

---

## Equipment
The company may provide:
- Laptop computers
- Monitors
- Accessories
- Approved software tools

Company-issued equipment remains company property.

---

## Expense Reimbursement
The company complies with California reimbursement requirements for necessary business expenses associated with remote work.

Employees should follow the Expense Reimbursement Policy for submission procedures.

---

## Security Requirements
Employees must:
- Use company-approved systems
- Enable MFA
- Use VPN access when required
- Protect confidential company information

---

## Work Location Restrictions
Employees may not permanently work outside California without prior approval due to tax, payroll, and compliance requirements.

---

## Performance Expectations
Remote and hybrid employees are held to the same performance standards as on-site employees.

Managers may modify or revoke remote work privileges if expectations are not met.

---

## Questions
Questions regarding hybrid or remote work arrangements should be directed to Human Resources.
