# Business Travel Policy – California Employees

## Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for safe, reasonable, and cost-effective business travel.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all California employees traveling on behalf of the company.

The company operates primarily on-site in San Diego, California, with approved hybrid work arrangements available.

---

## Travel Authorization
All business travel requires advance manager approval.

International travel may require executive approval.

---

## Airfare Guidelines
Employees should:
- Book economy class airfare
- Select reasonable flight options
- Book travel as early as practical

Business or first-class airfare requires executive approval.

---

## Hotel Guidelines
Hotel reimbursement is limited to:

- $200 per night before taxes and fees

Employees should:
- Select safe accommodations
- Stay near the business destination when practical
- Use reasonable judgment regarding lodging costs

Exceptions require prior approval.

---

## Daily Meal Allowance
Employees are eligible for reimbursement up to:

- $125 per person, per day

The allowance includes:
- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner
- Reasonable gratuities

Alcohol is generally not reimbursable unless approved for client entertainment or company events.

---

## Ground Transportation
Acceptable transportation includes:
- Rental cars
- Rideshare services
- Public transportation
- Taxis

Employees should select cost-effective options whenever practical.

---

## Mileage Reimbursement
Employees using personal vehicles for business travel will be reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate unless otherwise required by California law.

---

## Combining Business & Personal Travel
Employees may combine business and personal travel if:
- Approved in advance
- No additional company cost is incurred
- Personal expenses are separated clearly

---

## Safety Expectations
Employees should:
- Follow local laws
- Exercise reasonable caution
- Use reputable transportation providers
- Report emergencies promptly

---

## Expense Reporting
Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days following completion of travel.

Receipts are required for reimbursable expenses consistent with company policy.

---

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Examples include:
- Personal entertainment
- Family member travel
- Unapproved upgrades
- Traffic violations
- Personal expenses unrelated to business travel

---

## Policy Violations
Failure to comply with this policy may result in denied reimbursement or disciplinary action.

---

## Questions
Questions regarding travel or reimbursement should be directed to Finance or Human Resources.
